# Changelog

All notable changes to this module are listed here. The release workflow uses the section for the tagged version as the release notes.

## [0.1.0]

First release.

- Death detection on the third failed death save or the Dead status, or recorded by hand; the token is hidden and a grey, empty body is left where the character fell.
- Five tiers of glitches, one per death: Flicker (roleplay), Bleed (a fair swap), Drift, Fracture, Unraveling.
- The Wheel on the player's screen: a d20 (1 worse, 20 lighter, Heroic Inspiration rerolls), then a choice between two glitches that fit the character; the GM confirms, rerolls, swaps or rewrites.
- The return: held until the combat ends; full HP with spent resources, fully rested, or after the next long rest; placed where the GM clicks; a whisper to the player and a glitch screen for everyone.
- Raised before the return (no glitch), another character (hand the player a different actor), or cancel.
- Glitches as dnd5e features in their own group, with Active Effects for the numbers; ⟲ badges on everything born of or changed by a death, ◈ on the nexus, a death counter in the sheet header.
- Mend (exact undo, items keep their ids), Recover (items left on the body), and End the glitch (keep or release each one).
- Glitch Tracker journal page (a Planar Codex note when the Codex is installed), with memory fragments filled in from the console.
- About 35 glitches in English and Greek; JSON packs add personal glitches and GM reminders.
- Optional Planar Soundboard pads for death and return; macro API and hooks.
- English and Greek.
